# A Voice in the Forest: Aelin, the Last Elf
*Deep in the heart of Cindersap Forest, one may hear the faintest of songs, but no one knows where it comes from. However, one whose heart is pure, who can see the spirits of nature, may find the source of the song...*

This mod adds a new potential bachelor to Stardew Valley, and ties into past lore of the game! Meet **Aelin**, a beautiful and frail elf who has lived in Cindersap Forest for centuries. With the connection between human and nature beginning to crumble, however, his lifeforce has grown weak, and he has taken to hiding away in his little cottage away from human eyes. But, perhaps a farmer, newly arrived in Pelican Town, can restore his vitality and encourage him to face humans once more...?
